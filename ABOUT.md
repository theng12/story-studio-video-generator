# Story Studio Video Generator — what it is

Manifest V3 Chrome extension, video-only. Version 1.3.31 · published as `video-1.3.31` on
`theng12/kh-extensions` (sha256 `15d2b3903890…`, 81,337 B) · Extension category, line X001.

## What it does

- **Standalone (default):** takes local video prompts, generates through the signed-in Google Flow
  workspace, saves finished files to Downloads. Text-to-video (`YhhmEf`) and image-to-video (`MZZa6b`)
  are separate Flow RPCs with separate model keys.
- **Gateway Studio:** claims video jobs from Gateway Studio and reports them through the same queue.
  Worker contract v1, bearer token per worker; the extension only makes outbound calls and opens no port.
  Claims poll every 30 s while idle and unpaused in gateway mode.
- The queue is persistent, sequential and deduplicated, and survives panel closure. A paid submission
  whose outcome is unknown is never replayed; retained media is never regenerated to retry a delivery.
- Spends the Flow account's credits — this is a paid surface.

## How it is built

Plain MV3 files under `src/`, no build step; `node --test` suites under `test/`. The published zip is
`manifest.json` + `src/` from the tagged commit.

## Where it sits

Owned by Developer - Extension (X001). Its index row is generated by
`_orchestra/developer-extensions/rebuild-index.mjs` from the served zips and delivered to the signed
feed by the System lane. Source stays private; video's history holds an old tailnet address.

## Not established here

Live Flow generation and a real Gateway job have not been exercised from this checkout; they need the
owner's paid Flow session and a running Gateway.
